# Research integrity and current limitations

AuditCal is an experimental Python checker for **manually structured** statistical claims and **externally supplied** numerical evidence. The core software cannot parse arbitrary papers, infer model equivalence, perform causal identification or autonomously rerun arbitrary AI programs.

## Verified computational work
- 8 authentic AI-generated reports and source scripts preserved with source hashes.
- 24 numerical claims selected under a frozen report-level procedure. Initial independent analyses recorded 10 count agreements, 8 straightforward numerical inferential agreements, and 6 method/rounding/estimand cases requiring expert adjudication.
- A separate original AI-generated Soccer report had two data-source count inconsistencies under a documented source/filter. No population-level accuracy or error rate follows from individual examples.
- Source data have SHA-256 provenance checks. Original agent-authored programs were not executed by independent research scripts.

## What is NOT established
- No independent qualified blind human gold labels, no validated accuracy, sensitivity, precision/recall, false alarm rate, or claimed superiority to a baseline.
- Model identifiers are supplied by users; literal equality is not scientific comparability.
- Prior studies already audit agent analyses, agent decisions, code/prose links and statistical inconsistency. Novelty remains an open research question.
- The exploratory frozen 24-case pilot is not a prospective untouched test for checker logic developed after the analyses.
- Source-based numerical agreement does not prove appropriate research design, causation or substantive truth.

## Next scientific step
Secure independent methodological assessment for the six ambiguous cases, create independently annotated prospective examples with separation at dataset/task level, and run paired source-backed comparisons with confidence intervals and explicit abstention coverage.
